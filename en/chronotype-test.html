Your chronotype

Your chronotype describes when your inner clock makes you alert and when it makes you tired. It is largely innate and shifts over a lifetime: it gets later in adolescence and earlier again with age. No type is better than another; what matters is how well your daily life fits it.

Based on: findings from chronobiology on morning and evening types (for example Horne and Östberg, 1976) and on social jet lag (Wittmann, Dinich, Merrow and Roenneberg, 2006). The twelve questions are Ordinum's own wording and have not yet been scientifically validated. Evening tendency is the average of eleven questions; social jet lag is roughly estimated from your wake-up times on free days and on days with commitments. The daily curve is an illustration, not a measurement. Ordinum is meant for self-understanding and does not replace sleep medicine or psychological assessment.
