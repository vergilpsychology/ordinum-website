Based on: the public-domain IPIP Interpersonal Circumplex (IPIP-IPC) by Markey and Markey (2009) from the International Personality Item Pool. Eight styles with four statements each; the style names are deliberately chosen to be neutral. The two axes, taking the lead and closeness, are derived from all eight style scores after subtracting your personal average. Scores show your answers on a scale from 0 to 100, not comparisons with a population sample. Ordinum is meant for self-understanding and does not replace psychological assessment.
