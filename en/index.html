You think in possibilities and need room to act on them.

High openness with curiosity as your top strength means new ideas give you energy. Your conscientiousness sits in the middle range. Fixed plans help you as long as they leave you room. When they get too tight, projects tend to stall.

Try thisGive a new project a fixed, short block of 45 minutes a day and only decide in that moment what exactly you will work on. That way you combine structure with the freedom you need.
