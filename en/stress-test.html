When stress gets to be too much

Your answers suggest that stress is weighing on you heavily right now. That is not a diagnosis, but it is a reason to take good care of yourself. Talk to people you trust, or to your family doctor. In Germany, 116117 can arrange an appointment for an initial psychotherapy consultation, and the TelefonSeelsorge crisis line is free, anonymous and available around the clock at 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222. Elsewhere, your local crisis line or doctor can help.
